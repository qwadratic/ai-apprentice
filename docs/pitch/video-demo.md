# Product walkthrough: the demo video

The submission's demo video covers the problem, the solution and the UI (TASK-3.19).

- **Length.** Target 2:00, with a 60-second cut below. Confirm the length rule in Discord before the final edit.
- **Footage.** Record the real product: the web app at https://qwadratic.github.io/clipa/ and the API on the exe.dev VM.
- **Cases.** These follow stream A's DEMO-REAL-3 design: real Gmail and real Google Sheets, and an expert who departs from the usual workflow for a reason no screen shows.
- **People and data.** Ivan plays the expert and the teammate plays the new hire. All customers, amounts and accounts are synthetic. Say both on screen.

## Story

- **Problem.** The expert's judgment lives in her head. A screen recording shows *what* she did, never *why*.
- **Solution.** Clipa is an apprentice. She watches the expert work, asks why at natural pauses, has the expert confirm what she understood, and then coaches the next person on a new case.
- **UI.** Three stages: **Show → Reflect → Pass it on.** Clipa, the teal paperclip, is the guide throughout.

## How to record (no new tooling needed)

1. **Record the 07:35 dry run.** On the Mac, use Cmd+Shift+5, "Record entire screen", with the internal microphone **off**: Clipa's voice comes through the system audio, and the voice-over is added later. The raw footage is then ready before the pitch.
2. **Record the voice-over separately**, one take per row, in QuickTime → New Audio Recording.
3. **Cut it in any editor** (iMovie is enough):
   - keep Clipa's real lines from the recording;
   - put the voice-over under the problem and solution shots;
   - add the captions from the table.
4. **Fallback.** If Clipa misses a question in a take, record that stage again. Never dub a line Clipa did not say. The video must show the real product.
